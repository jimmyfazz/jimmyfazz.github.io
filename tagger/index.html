    How it works. Add this page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and always open it from there: it then works without signal and keeps your taps even if the phone locks. Tap Kick-off on the whistle, then each moment as it happens — a few seconds late is fine. After a goal for us, tap the scorer’s shirt number (or “Not sure”). At the end, Send to the reel maker and choose WhatsApp, Messages, AirDrop or email.

    Privacy. Shirt numbers only — no names. The taps stay on this phone until you send them; this page can’t send anything anywhere by itself.
